In Fall 2020 I taught Combustion Processes (AER315) at U of T. It happened to be the first time the course ran online, so I rebuilt the slides and notes to work over a screen. The handouts I wrote are linked on the course page below, so feel free to use them if they help.

I've also been a teaching assistant for calculus, thermodynamics, measurements and combustion courses at U of T and IIT Bombay. My favorite part of teaching is starting from something everyday, like a candle flame, and taking it apart until the physics shows.
