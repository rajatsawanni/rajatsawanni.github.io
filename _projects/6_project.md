### Overview

As a dual-degree (B.Tech. and M.Tech.) student in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay, I worked on two independent research projects in thermo-fluid sciences, one experimental and one numerical. This work received the **Undergraduate Research Award** at IIT Bombay (2017).

---

## Development of gas–liquid rocket engines (2016–2018)

_Advisor: Prof. Arindrajit Chowdhury, IIT Bombay_

I contributed to the development and operation of a gas–liquid rocket engine test facility, including the design, fabrication and characterization of multiple single-element injector configurations, to evaluate novel fuels and advance engine technologies. This was the subject of my M.Tech. thesis, _Injector design and characterizations for gas–liquid rocket engine_.

- Shaikh, T., Sawanni, R., Chowdhury, A., Umakant, S. (2017). A comparison of the performance of uni-element injectors for laboratory scale gas–liquid rocket engines. _11th Asia-Pacific Conference on Combustion_, Sydney, Australia.

---

## Flow-induced reconfiguration of aquatic vegetation (2015–2016)

_Advisor: Prof. Rajneesh Bhardwaj, IIT Bombay_

I studied the deformation of flexible cilia using an in-house fluid–structure interaction solver, and extended this work into a theoretical framework for flow-induced reconfiguration of aquatic vegetation in low-Reynolds-number viscous flows.

- Bhati, A.\*, Sawanni, R.\*, Kulkarni, K., Bhardwaj, R. (2018). Role of skin friction drag during flow-induced reconfiguration of a flexible thin plate. _Journal of Fluids and Structures_, 77, 134–150. [doi:10.1016/j.jfluidstructs.2017.12.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfluidstructs.2017.12.003)
