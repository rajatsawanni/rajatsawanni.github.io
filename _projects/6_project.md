Before coming to Toronto, I did a dual degree (B.Tech. and M.Tech.) in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay. Along the way I worked on two quite different research projects, one in the lab and one on the computer.

## Gas–liquid rocket engines (2016–2018)

_With Prof. Arindrajit Chowdhury_

I helped develop and run a gas–liquid rocket engine test facility, and designed, built and tested several single-element injectors to compare how they perform with different fuels. This became my M.Tech. thesis.

- Shaikh, T., Sawanni, R., Chowdhury, A., Umakant, S. (2017). A comparison of the performance of uni-element injectors for laboratory scale gas–liquid rocket engines. _11th Asia-Pacific Conference on Combustion_, Sydney, Australia.

## How flexible plants bend in a flow (2015–2016)

_With Prof. Rajneesh Bhardwaj_

Using an in-house fluid–structure interaction solver, I simulated how flexible cilia and thin plates deform in slow, viscous flows, and built that into a simple theory for how aquatic vegetation reconfigures in a current.

- Bhati, A.\*, Sawanni, R.\*, Kulkarni, K., Bhardwaj, R. (2018). Role of skin friction drag during flow-induced reconfiguration of a flexible thin plate. _Journal of Fluids and Structures_, 77, 134–150. [doi:10.1016/j.jfluidstructs.2017.12.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfluidstructs.2017.12.003)
