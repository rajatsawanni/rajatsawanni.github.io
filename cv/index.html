Professional Summary

Experimental combustion and aerosol scientist working on soot formation at high pressure, contrail formation and ice nucleation, emissions of sustainable aviation fuels and hydrogen, and optical diagnostics with radiative-transfer and reaction-network modeling.

Experience

Diagnostics and modeling: laser and optical diagnostics of reacting and multiphase flows, radiative transfer in sooting flames, flame synthesis of carbon nanomaterials
