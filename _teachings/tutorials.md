*Jul 2017 – Dec 2023*

Responsibilities included leading problem-solving sessions, assisting with laboratory and computational exercises, preparing assessment materials, managing learning management systems, and providing office-hour support.

**University of Toronto**

Delivered tutorials covering differential calculus, limits, continuity, optimization, and engineering applications of derivatives. Assisted students in developing analytical problem-solving techniques and mathematical foundations required for engineering studies.

## Calculus II (ESC195)
**University of Toronto**

Conducted tutorial sessions on integral calculus, series expansions, multivariable calculus, and introductory differential equations. Guided students through both theoretical concepts and practical engineering applications.

**University of Toronto**
